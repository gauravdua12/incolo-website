Our mission

To make AI genuinely useful in daily plant operations — fast, private and agentic — helping manufacturers automate inspection, predict failures, plan production and monitor the floor in real time, without ever exposing process or quality data to the public cloud.

Manufacturing AI programs

Focused, time-boxed pilots that prove value in weeks

Every program follows the same disciplined path — Assess & Design → Pilot & Validate → Scale & Govern — with clear quality gates and a measurable business case at each stage.

AI Visual Inspection

Pharma · Automotive · Discrete

Edge-deployed computer vision trained on line-specific defect libraries — surface, dimensional, print and assembly-completeness — with sub-second inference and a human-in-the-loop review queue.

  • Reduced escaped-defect rate and less end-of-line manual sampling
  • Audit-ready defect trail suitable for GMP and OEM reviews

Packaging & Bin-Count Verification

Discrete & Batch · Pre-Dispatch QC

Vision-based AI counts each piece as it is placed and checks the final count against target before a container is sealed — with an auditable photo record and a logged override for exceptions.

  • Miscounts caught at the packing station in seconds, not at the customer dock
  • Photo-backed proof of what shipped, with no added paperwork

Predictive Maintenance

Rotating Equipment · Utilities · Critical Assets

Vibration, temperature, current and pressure data streamed to on-premise ML models trained on asset-specific failure signatures — with remaining-useful-life alerts and CMMS/EAM work-order integration.

  • Fewer unplanned stoppages on critical lines and utilities
  • Shift from calendar-based to condition-based maintenance

Production Planning

Multi-Product · Batch & Discrete

Agentic re-planning that proposes revised schedules when an order changes — with a plain-language interface so planners can request a build plan or dispatch report, checked against the BOM and live stock before anything is committed.

  • Improved on-time delivery against customer commitments
  • Minutes, not hours, to turn a request into an approval-ready order

Real-Time Shop-Floor Monitoring

Multi-Line Visibility · OEE

Real-time OEE, throughput and quality dashboards built on your existing SCADA/MES data, deployed on-premise — with an AI copilot that flags anomalies as they emerge and Andon-style escalation.

  • Slowdowns caught within the hour, not at shift-end
  • Single real-time view of OEE across lines and shifts

Brazing Furnace Optimization

Automotive Thermal · Heat-Exchangers

ML models trained on furnace zone temperatures, belt speed and atmosphere against downstream leak-test results — recommending parameter adjustments to hold the optimal thermal profile as load and product mix vary.

  • Reduced scrap and rework from weak or over-brazed joints
  • Lower energy per unit through tighter thermal-profile control
